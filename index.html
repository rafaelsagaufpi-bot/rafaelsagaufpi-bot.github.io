01 / ABOUT

Research at the intersection of theory and computation.

I am a researcher in a research group of Federal University of Piauí (UFPI) with a background in physics, including graduate studies at UFPI and doctoral studies at the University of São Paulo (USP).

My interests span theoretical and mathematical physics, statistical mechanics, stochastic processes, mathematical reasoning, and scientific computing.

This website is a living portfolio of research directions, publications, and software projects.
